# Helix Systems Employee Handbook

*Fictional sample document created for the Enterprise Knowledge Copilot demo. Helix Systems is not a real company.*

Version 4.2 — Effective 1 April 2026. Owner: People Operations.

## 1. Working Hours

The standard work week at Helix Systems is 40 hours, Monday to Friday. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST, during which all employees are expected to be available for meetings. Outside core hours employees may choose their own start and finish times with their manager's agreement.

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from the department head and People Operations.

## 2. Leave Policy

### 2.1 Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave accrues monthly at a rate of 2 days per month. Employees in their first year of employment receive annual leave on a pro-rata basis from their joining date.

Annual leave requests of up to 3 consecutive days must be submitted at least 7 days in advance through the HR portal. Requests longer than 3 consecutive days must be submitted at least 30 days in advance and approved by the reporting manager.

Up to 10 days of unused annual leave may be carried forward to the next calendar year. Carried-forward leave expires if it is not used by 31 March of the following year. Unused leave above the carry-forward limit lapses at year end and is not paid out, except on resignation, when accrued but unused annual leave is encashed at the basic salary rate.

### 2.2 Casual Leave

Employees receive 8 days of casual leave per calendar year for short, unplanned personal needs. Casual leave cannot be carried forward and cannot be combined with annual leave to extend a vacation. No more than 2 consecutive days of casual leave may be taken at a time.

### 2.3 Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days. Unused sick leave may accumulate up to a maximum of 30 days.

### 2.4 Parental Leave

Birthing parents are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Non-birthing parents are entitled to 4 weeks of paid paternity leave, which must be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption. Adoptive parents of a child under 3 months receive 12 weeks of paid leave.

### 2.5 Public Holidays

Helix Systems observes 12 public holidays per year. The holiday calendar is published by People Operations every December for the following year.

## 3. Probation

New employees serve a probation period of 6 months. During probation the notice period is 15 days. Probation may be extended once by up to 3 months if performance objectives are not met.

## 4. Notice Period and Resignation

After confirmation, the notice period is 60 days for individual contributors and 90 days for managers and above. Notice can be shortened by mutual agreement or bought out by the employee at the basic salary rate.

## 5. Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in April and October. Ratings use a five-point scale. Salary revisions take effect from 1 July following the April review cycle.

## 6. Code of Conduct

Employees must treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination and retaliation are not tolerated. Concerns can be raised confidentially through the Ethics Hotline or directly with People Operations.
