# Helix Systems Information Security Policy

*Fictional sample document created for the Enterprise Knowledge Copilot demo.*

Version 2.1 — Owner: Information Security Office (ISO).

## 1. Passwords and Authentication

Passwords for company accounts must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused across the last 10 passwords. Passwords must be changed every 180 days or immediately if a compromise is suspected.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN, source-code repositories and all cloud consoles. Hardware security keys are required for administrators of production systems.

## 2. Devices

Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and the endpoint protection agent managed by IT. Laptops lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Personal devices may access company email only through the approved mobile device management (MDM) app.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 1 hour of discovery so the device can be remotely wiped.

## 3. Data Classification

Information is classified into four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Restricted data includes customer personal data, payment information and authentication secrets. Restricted data must never be stored on removable media or shared through personal email or messaging apps.

## 4. Incident Reporting

Any suspected security incident, including phishing emails, malware alerts or accidental data disclosure, must be reported to security@helix.example within 30 minutes. Do not attempt to investigate an incident yourself or delete evidence.

The security team classifies incidents by severity. Severity 1 incidents trigger the incident response team within 15 minutes, 24 hours a day.

## 5. Access Management

Access follows the principle of least privilege. Access reviews for production systems are performed every quarter. When an employee leaves, all accounts are disabled on their last working day.

## 6. Acceptable Use

Company systems may not be used for cryptocurrency mining, unlicensed software, or any activity that violates the law. Use of unapproved generative AI tools with Confidential or Restricted data is prohibited; employees must use the approved internal AI assistant instead.
